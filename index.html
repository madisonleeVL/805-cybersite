// About

Grassroots since day one.

805 Cyber started in 2018 with a simple idea: the people protecting the Central Coast should actually know each other. Nearly a hundred meetups later, that's still the whole point.

We're CISOs and SOC analysts, pentesters and GRC leads, students breaking in and veterans who've seen it all. We trade what's working, what isn't, and who's hiring. Over a drink, usually with an ocean view.

What this isn't

  • A sales floorNo pitching unless someone asks. Vendors are welcome as people, not as booths.
  • A badge scanYour email gets event updates. That's it. Never sold, never shared.

// What we believe

Three rules. That's it.

01

Give first.

The best way to get help is to offer it. Share what you know, make the intro, pass along the job.

02

Show up.

Trust gets built in person, one conversation at a time. Nearly a hundred meetups in, we still show up.

03

Keep it local.

From Santa Barbara to Westlake, the people defending your neighbors' networks are your neighbors.
